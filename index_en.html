FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can stay?
+
Up to 8 guests. The house is reserved exclusively for your group — no other guests. Ideal for families, friend groups, and workationers. Splitting the cost across a group makes it very affordable per person.
How do I get from Nagasaki Airport to Yohaku?
+
Approximately 15 minutes by car. Free on-site parking is available. We recommend renting a car at the airport for the most convenient access. Detailed directions and public transport options will be provided upon booking confirmation.
Is Yohaku suitable for workationers?
+
Yes. Stable Wi-Fi and a dedicated workspace are included. The calm environment and natural surroundings make it ideal for focused remote work. Extended stays are welcome.
Are meals provided?
+
Breakfast and dinner are not provided. The house has a fully equipped kitchen — cooking with local ingredients from the nearby supermarket is a highlight for many guests. Local restaurants are also within easy reach by car.
Is there a multi-night discount?
+
Yes. Book via the official site and stay 2 or more nights — the second night onward is 10% off. We recommend 2–3 nights to fully explore Nagasaki city, the Hasami & Arita pottery towns, and Unzen hot springs.
What are the nearby attractions?
+
Nagasaki city (Glover Garden, Dejima, Chinatown, Mt. Inasa night view), Huis Ten Bosch (~30 min), Hasami & Arita pottery towns (~40 min), Unzen hot springs (~90 min). A local sightseeing guide is provided at check-in.
